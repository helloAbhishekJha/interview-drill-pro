Privacy policy — Interview Drill Pro

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Interview Drill Pro (“the app”) is an offline practice app for manager conversations and interview drills. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

Data stored on your device

Drill history (the prompts you practiced and when) is saved only on your phone, using local storage. The app does not run its own server and does not upload that history. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Purchases

Paid access (“Pro”) is offered as a Google Play subscription. Google Play processes the payment. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Google Play so the app can tell whether Pro is active. That receipt can include a store user id and subscription status. It does not include your full card or UPI details.

A reviewer promo code, if you enter one, is checked inside the app and stored only on that device. It is not sent to a server.

What we do not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or advertising id. It does not use analytics or crash SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/helloAbhishekJha/interview-drill-pro or contact the developer via the Google Play listing once published.
