## What every entry must build

1. **Brand-new app** — first public store release during Aug 1–Sep 30, 2026 (updates to old apps do **not** qualify).
2. **RevenueCat SDK** — at least one in-app or web purchase **or** ads via **RevenueCat Ads**.
3. **Platform** — iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android; published on App Store, Google Play, or Samsung Galaxy Store.
4. **US-accessible** store listing.

## Category summaries & judging criteria

### Grand Prize ($100k)

**Intent:** App with the most **user traction and growth momentum** during the event — post-release growth story matters more than raw revenue alone.

**Shortlisting:** RevenueCat total revenue during submission period creates a **shortlist**; highest revenue does **not** auto-win.

**Judging criteria (official rules):**

1. **Early and effective release** — When you shipped a live, usable version; why then; how early build enabled traction testing.
2. **Growth by numbers** — Product improvements, acquisition, retention, marketing experiments after launch, linked to concrete results (installs, active users, paying customers, conversion, retention, MRR/ARR, social reach, etc.) and what you'd do next.

**Extra Devpost field:** Post-launch growth description + relevant numbers.

---

### #BuildInPublic ($30k / $20k / $10k)

**Intent:** Most interesting **public development journey** on social media.

**Requirements:** Links to social accounts/posts; short text on how building in public helped the app. Posts should tag **#Shipaton** (may also use #BuildInPublic).

**Judging criteria:**

1. **Sharing your story** — How much and how creatively you shared the process (audience size does **not** matter).
2. **Engagement** — Community response; ideas incorporated from feedback.
3. **Lessons learned** — How public sharing improved the app.

---

### HAMM — Help Apps Make Money ($20k / $10k / $5k)

**Intent:** Smartest use of RevenueCat to drive **real revenue** — paywall, pricing, packaging, conversion.

**Extra Devpost field:** Monetization strategy, paywall/pricing, conversion/revenue numbers.

**Judging criteria:**

1. Revenue streams articulated and integrated into design; realistic, scalable strategy for target market.
2. Innovative or diverse monetization beyond a single standard model.
3. Sustainable business model; differentiation vs competitors; ability to generate revenue long-term.

---

### RevenueCat Design Award ($20k / $10k / $5k)

**Intent:** Best **craft** of app development — separate from business viability.

**Extra Devpost field:** Unique design elements; what judges should look for (animations, UX).

**Judging criteria:**

1. **Innovative ideas** — Technical or UX innovations, unique experiences, animations.
2. **Aesthetics** — Delightful to look at and use; smooth gestures; beautiful art.

---

### RevenueCat Peace Prize ($20k / $10k / $5k)

**Intent:** Greatest **social good** — benefits to communities or society.

**Extra Devpost field:** How the app benefits individuals, communities, or society.

**Judging criteria:**

1. **Impact** — Clear demonstration of benefit.
2. **Feasibility** — Realistic technology; potential reach (many people or high impact on smaller group).

---

### Catvertising Award ($20k / $10k / $5k)

**Intent:** Most creative **RevenueCat Ads** monetization — clever placements, smart stack integration, experience users don't hate.

**Extra Devpost field:** How RC Ads are used; placements; integration with subscriptions/IAP.

**Judging criteria:**

1. Ads integrated naturally — useful/additive vs interruptive; help users access richer experience.
2. Ad strategy fits audience, use case, and broader revenue model alongside subs/trials/IAP.

---

### Best Game Award ($20k / $10k / $5k)

**Intent:** Best **mobile game** — gameplay, art direction, monetization fit for genre.

**Extra Devpost field:** Gameplay, art, monetization model fit.

**Judging criteria:**

1. Fun and engaging?
2. Unique gameplay, progression, replayability?
3. How is the game monetized?

---

### Next Gen Award ($20k / $10k / $5k) — students only

**Intent:** Best app by **active students** (high school, college, bootcamp, etc.).

**Different path:** Demo video + **public open-source repo** — **no paid store account required**. `.edu` or equivalent email; minors 13+ need parent/guardian consent.

**Judging criteria:**

1. Clear, useful, original idea?
2. Meaningful progress toward working app (video + code)?
3. Thoughtful RevenueCat use?
4. Technical choices and product thinking?

## Submission requirements checklist

### All categories (except Next Gen)

- [ ] Text description of features and functionality
- [ ] **Demo video ≤2 minutes** (YouTube/Vimeo, public) — device footage; judges not required to watch past 2 min
- [ ] **Store URL** (App Store / Google Play / Galaxy Store) — US accessible
- [ ] **1024×1024** app icon
- [ ] **≥1 screenshot** at **1179×2556** px, **no device frame**
- [ ] **Free trial OR promo code** for judges to test all premium features
- [ ] RevenueCat project integrated (IAP/sub or RC Ads)
- [ ] First public release **Aug 1 – Sep 30, 2026**
