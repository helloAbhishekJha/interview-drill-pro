## Common patterns across winners

### 1. App types judges favor

- **Productivity & life tools** — task managers, focus, reminders, photo cleanup, travel (Karo, Flowmino, Echo, Shutter Declutter, Tomo)
- **EdTech & skill-building** — Gurwi, SkillMe, Studient, PitchLab (learning + measurable progress)
- **Niche vertical depth** — not “another todo app”; clear wedge (class-action cash, tick ID, pitch tracking, BJJ, party games)
- **Consumer utility with obvious value** — Payout, Vector Guard, Dayloop
- **Less common as Grand Prize:** pure games (separate Best Game category in 2026), meta hackathon tools

### 2. Polish signals

- **Demo video ≤2 min** showing real device footage, paywall, and core loop (judges not required to watch past 2 min)
- **Store listing quality** — ASO keywords (Oshizan 2026 entrant did keyword research pre-launch); professional screenshots without device frames at required resolution
- **Micro-interactions** — haptics, animations (Flowmino breathing, Dayloop ghost overlay)
- **Offline / privacy** when relevant (Vector Guard, Dayloop local photos) — judges call this out explicitly
- **Named award intent in Devpost + video** — 2026 Week 7 update: *“Judges shouldn't have to guess what they're assessing”*

### 3. RevenueCat integration depth

- **Beyond “RC is installed”:** Blinkist-style paywalls (Karo), goal-adaptive paywall copy (SkillMe), hybrid credits + sub (Napkinmatic, Studient)
- **HAMM winners articulate *why* pricing fits the market** — one-sentence model (Vector Guard’s 1:50 line)
- **Free tier that demonstrates value, paywall at collaboration/power features** (Karo)
- **Document conversion/revenue numbers** even if small — Voicetree ($2k), ReadHim ($1.1k MRR) cited
- **Judge testing path:** promo code or trial mandatory; broken IAP = ineligible

### 4. #BuildInPublic / social traction

- **Grand Prize 2025 = traction**, but **#BuildInPublic 2025 = story** — Gurwi won BiP with 13k users *and* documented journey
- Daily or near-daily posts (#Shipaton tag in 2026); YouTube devlogs (Meshing, Friendy+ series)
- **Community feedback changing the app** — Meshing features from Twitter replies; Echo user testing in coffee shops
- **Authentic founder narrative** — Tomo Japan career switch; Gurwi Colombia education gap
- BiP alone rarely wins Grand Prize without numbers; Grand Prize without public story still wins (Payout)

### 5. Prize category splits (evolution)

| Year | Headline categories | Sponsor / specialty |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| 2024 | Make Money, Design, BuildInPublic | — |
| 2025 | Grand ($65k), BiP, Design, Buzziest Launch, HAMM, Peace, Best Vibes | OneSignal, Kotlin MP, Staff |
| 2026 | Grand ($100k), BiP, HAMM, Design, Peace, Best Game, Catvertising, Next Gen | OneSignal (Keep Them Coming Back), JetBrains KMP, Noise, Samsung Galaxy, Replit, Layers, Stripe, **6 Influencer awards** |

**Realistic targets for a solo Android Expo app without launch budget:** HAMM, Design (if UX is exceptional), **Career Coaching Influencer**, #BuildInPublic, **OneSignal combo** — **not** Grand Prize or Most Viral (Noise) without marketing spend. (**Buzziest Launch was 2025 only.**)

## Map to Interview Drill Pro

### Strengths aligned with past winners

| Your feature | Winner parallel |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Offline FAANG behavioral drills | Vector Guard offline-first; Momental no-login simplicity |
| Timed drill loop (2+5 min) | SkillMe daily challenge structure; PitchLab measurable practice |
| STAR / manager frameworks | SkillMe structured skill paths; Heartbeat Hero training modes |
| Free daily prompt + Pro decks | Karo free tier → pay at collaboration; Gurwi free engagement → sub |
| No backend | Faster ship; judges reward focus (Momental “one page, one tap”) |
| Android + Expo | Apol won 2024 Design on Android; Studient/Gurwi cross-platform EdTech |

### Copy from winners

1. **One-line monetization story for HAMM** — e.g. *“One Pro subscription unlocks offline FAANG decks; free tier builds the daily habit that converts before onsite season.”*
2. **Goal-adaptive paywall copy** (SkillMe) — tailor RC offering description to “Google L4 behavioral” vs “Meta system design lite”
3. **Blinkist-style paywall** (Karo) — hero benefit + secondary “View all plans”
4. **Judge promo code + 2-min video** showing: daily free drill → timer → paywall → unlock Pro deck → history
5. **#BuildInPublic thread** — ship Play listing early, post daily drill tips + build updates with #Shipaton
6. **Enter Career Coaching Influencer category** — frame manager/behavioral decks as “practice hard conversations before the real interview”
7. **Document numbers in Devpost** — sessions completed, Play installs, trial→paid if any (even n=small)

### Avoid

1. **Positioning as generic AI flashcard app** — Studient/VoiceTree territory; lead with *timed offline drill*, not AI generation
2. **Chasing Grand Prize without growth** — needs post-launch traction narrative; revenue shortlists but doesn’t auto-win
3. **Most Viral (Noise) / ReadHim-style stunts** — out of scope for Sep 27 submit (Buzziest Launch was 2025 only)
4. **Under-documenting RC** — mock mode in demo video; missing project ID or promo code
5. **iOS-polish bias assumption** — compensate with **crisp Android screenshots**, Material-consistent UI, explicit “works offline on Pixel” in video
6. **Stealth submit** — 2024 Meshing and 2025 Gurwi show BiP feedback materially improved winners
7. **Peace Prize framing** unless genuine social-good angle (job access for underrepresented candidates) with specific beneficiary story

### Suggested award targets (priority)

1. **HAMM** — subscription + clear free/pro split + written monetization rationale  
2. **Career Coaching Influencer (Leadership Heather)** — behavioral + manager decks  
3. **#BuildInPublic** — daily posts from now through Sep 30  
4. **RevenueCat Design** — only if timer UX, deck cards, and paywall get extra animation/polish pass  
5. **Grand Prize** — secondary; mention any Play installs/retention if you have them, don’t optimize solely for revenue in 4 weeks
